About Slangify

Why this website was created

Slangify was created to help English learners understand the language people actually use in messages, social media, games and everyday conversations. Its goal is to make slang, abbreviations and spoken phrases easier to learn through short explanations, clear Kazakh translations, real examples and practice tests.
