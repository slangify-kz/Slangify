# Local video example

The first sample is an archival NASA Apollo 11 recording from 1969, demonstrating the conversational marker “okay”. It is not a movie scene.

- Original source and public-domain status: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apollo_11_Landing_-_first_steps_on_the_moon.ogv
- Credit: NASA.
- NASA media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- Excerpt: 35.800–42.600, 6.8 seconds.
- Caption: “Okay, I'm going to step off the LEM now.”
- Format: H.264 baseline / AAC, MP4 with fast start, for native mobile playback.

The original is identified as a US federal government work in the public domain. No NASA endorsement is implied. The public interface credits NASA in plain text. There are no third-party players, advertising widgets, or outgoing video links.

The one-time preparation workflow downloads the credited source, transcodes the excerpt, runs the content and study tests, and commits the clip plus its dictionary entry together. It runs only when that workflow or its preparation script changes. Normal word edits do not run this workflow.

Owner uploads must be original recordings, public-domain material, or material licensed for republication.
